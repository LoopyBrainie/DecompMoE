## Reading

The defect the entry names — "全体专家皆死时 SP 返回 `0.0`，与合法活测量
`SP = 0.0` 在输出层不可区分" — no longer exists. The all-dead path returns `NaN`,
which lies outside the declared range and therefore cannot collide with any live
measurement, and the module docstring states that `NaN` must never be reported as
`0.0` nor clamped or aggregated downstream.

The reason `0.0` specifically was unsafe is now documented rather than merely
avoided: per-token alignment terms can cancel exactly, so `0.0` is a value a
legitimate live measurement genuinely takes. The fix chose a sentinel that is
unreachable by the live path.
