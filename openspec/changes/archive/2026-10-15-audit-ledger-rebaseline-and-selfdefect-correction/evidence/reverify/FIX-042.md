## What the count means

Requirements permitted to lack a top-level `**Source:**` field, grandfathered
under `governance` `req-gov-11`. The lint enforces the registry in both
directions — an entry naming a Requirement that does not exist is reported as
*stale*, and so is one whose Requirement has since acquired a `**Source:**`
field. That two-way check is what makes the number meaningful rather than
decorative.

`decompmoe-skeleton` 23 — **74 total**, matching the gate's
`anchor coverage (74 anchor(s) across 3 capabilit(ies))` line. So "20/70" in the
entry uses 70; the current denominator is 74. The **numerator is unchanged**;
the ratio's denominator moved because four governance anchors were added since
the pin (three restored by `c568803`, and `req-gov-13`–`req-gov-16`).
